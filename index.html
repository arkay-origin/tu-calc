Formula settings

Defaults come from the community charts: Bolt's max-out costs and the lifetime requirement chart. Both use next = current × growth + base. If the dev changes the numbers, or an in-game check shows a base is off, correct it here.

Growth & rounding

On some rows Bolt's chart rounds up by 1 when a step lands exactly on a whole number, a side effect of computer decimal math. That puts it a few units high (under 0.01% by A110). Strict round-up uses exact math instead. Either way, the bank total always covers the lifetime requirement, so if you've saved the bank you're also allowed to ascend.
