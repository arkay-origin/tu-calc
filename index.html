Data & formula settings

Bank costs for A1–A110 are the dev's published figures, used exactly as given. Above A110 they're estimated with cost = factor × (growthn − 1), which matches the published table to within 0.03% from A20 up. Lifetime requirements use base × (growthn − 1) ÷ (growth − 1) and match the requirement chart exactly.
